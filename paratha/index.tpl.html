੧Step one · knead

The atta

Whole wheat flour, water and a pinch of salt, kneaded until the dough is soft and springy. Then it rests, so it rolls out later without tearing.

  • Whole wheat atta
  • Water
  • Salt
੨Step two · boil & mash

The aloo

Potatoes are boiled until tender, peeled while warm and mashed smooth. No lumps, so the filling spreads right to the edges.

  • Potatoes
  • Boiled
  • Mashed warm
੩Step three · spice

The masala

Green chilli, fresh coriander, onion, ajwain, red chilli and a little amchur for tang are mixed through the mash.

  • Green chilli
  • Coriander
  • Ajwain
  • Amchur
੪Step four · stuff

The pouch

A ball of dough is pressed into a cup, packed with filling and pleated shut. It has to be sealed with no gaps, so nothing leaks onto the tawa.

  • Press
  • Fill
  • Pleat & seal
੫Step five · roll

The belan

A dusting of flour, then the belan rolls it out slowly and evenly, so the aloo reaches every edge without breaking through.

  • Dry flour
  • Belan & chakla
੬Step six · cook

The tawa

Onto a hot iron tawa and flipped as it blisters. Brushed with ghee and flipped again, until both sides are golden with crisp brown spots.

  • Iron tawa
  • Ghee
  • Golden spots
੭Step seven · serve

The plate

Straight off the tawa with a slab of butter, plus dahi and achar on the side. Yours is one tap away.
